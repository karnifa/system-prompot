# Design Mode Manifest

Load this capability group when the user intent is primarily product, UI, UX, visual design, or prototyping.

## Agents

- `agents/design-planner.md`: chooses fidelity, audience, and design direction.
- `agents/wireframe-explorer.md`: generates multiple low-fidelity directions.
- `agents/interface-designer.md`: specifies polished screens, states, and interactions.

## Skills

- `skills/wireframe.md`: broad low-fidelity exploration.
- `skills/interface-design.md`: screen-level product design.
- `skills/design-system.md`: components, tokens, variants, and consistency.
- `skills/prototype.md`: interaction, state, and motion behavior.

## Activation

Activate this group automatically for screens, product flows, dashboards, app UI, visual polish, design systems, prototypes, and responsive layout.
